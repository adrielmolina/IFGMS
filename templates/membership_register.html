What is MAAB?

MAAB (Membership Accident Assistance Benefits) is a Red Cross Philippines program that provides emergency medical assistance, accident insurance, and financial support for hospitalization expenses to registered members.

To become a member, register now or just visit our nearest PRC Chapter in your area.

Terms and Conditions

By registering for the Membership and Accident Assistance Benefit (MAAB) program of the Philippine Red Cross (PRC), you agree to the following terms and conditions:

  1. Membership Validity — Valid for one (1) year from the date of issuance. Renewal must be done before expiration.
  2. Membership Fee and ID Claiming — Payment must be made upon claiming your MAAB ID at the nearest PRC Chapter.
  3. Accident Assistance Coverage — Benefits apply only to active members with valid MAAB IDs within the coverage period.
  4. Accuracy of Information — Members must ensure all provided details are correct. PRC is not liable for incorrect or falsified information.
  5. Non-transferability — Membership and benefits are strictly non-transferable.
  6. Lost or Damaged IDs — Replacement IDs may incur additional charges.

For more details, visit your nearest Philippine Red Cross Chapter.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

The MAAB Program is an initiative of the Philippine Red Cross that provides accident insurance and emergency medical benefits to its members. It allows individuals to support Red Cross humanitarian activities while receiving protection in case of accidents, death, or medical emergencies.

You can register by completing a membership form at your local Red Cross branch or using the membership registration online! After payment, your information will be encoded into the Membership Information System, and your MAAB ID will be issued by the Cavite Chapter.

You can visit the nearest local branch in your area, Cavite-Chapter(MAIN) in Cavite City, Silang branch, & Dasma branch. Show a screenshot of your confirmation upon register and pay, your MAAB ID will release on the spot!

Depending on your chosen membership type, benefits may include coverage for accident death, disablement, medical reimbursement, unprovoked assault, and daily hospital allowance.

Your MAAB ID serves as proof of membership and eligibility for Red Cross accident assistance benefits. The MAAB ID can be used when you're involved in an accident and need to claim insurance benefits or assistance.

How to claim benefits:

  • Contact your nearest Red Cross branch
  • Walk-in to any Red Cross office
